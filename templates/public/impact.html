How ShareMeal Validates Impact

Unlike theoretical impact calculators, ShareMeal maintains a rigorous chain of custody. A food donation only increments the quantity_distributed metric when:

  1. A verified donor publishes a listing with specific weight/portion values.
  2. An authorized receiving charity approves and requests the portion.
  3. A vetted volunteer confirms physical pickup from the commercial kitchen.
  4. The receiving organization supervisor executes a digital receipt confirmation acknowledging the food arrived intact and safe for distribution.

Social ROI for Commercial Kitchens

Donors receive detailed tax-deductible surplus redistribution certificates at year-end, along with verifiable ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) data on municipal waste diversion.

Nutritional Support for Shelters

Shelters and soup kitchens replace low-nutrient packaged staples with chef-prepared, balanced, high-protein surplus dishes, elevating dignity and health outcomes for community residents.
