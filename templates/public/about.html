Our Mission

Redistributing Wholesome Surplus to Nourish Communities

ShareMeal was founded with a single imperative: perfectly good food prepared in commercial kitchens should never end up in landfills while neighbors face hunger.

The Challenge: A Disconnected Supply Chain

Commercial food establishments — including hotels, banquet facilities, high-volume restaurants, supermarkets, and artisan bakeries — operate in environments with unavoidable demand variability. At the close of business or end of an event, large quantities of safe, freshly prepared food remain unconsumed.

Meanwhile, local emergency shelters, transitional housing facilities, and community kitchens struggle to stretch limited budgets to provide high-protein, balanced meals to the families they serve.

Historically, the barrier to connecting these two groups has been logistics, time pressure, and food safety uncertainty. ShareMeal removes these obstacles with structured protocols, role-based workflows, and volunteer coordination.

Food Safety First

We enforce strict cold-chain standards, allergen disclosures, and mandatory packaging declarations. Every listing has a hard expiration deadline, and any participant can raise an immediate safety freeze.

Transparent Verification

No entity participates without administrative verification. Commercial donors provide food health permits, receiving organizations provide 501(c)(3) or charitable registration, and volunteers provide ID verification.

Environmental Impact

Food waste accounts for approximately 8-10% of global greenhouse gas emissions. For every kilogram of cooked surplus food rescued and redirected to human consumption:

  • 2.5 kg of CO2 equivalent emissions are averted from decomposing in municipal landfills.
  • 450 liters of agricultural water embedded in crop production, processing, and refrigeration are preserved.
  • High-protein meals reach individuals without requiring additional food manufacturing footprints.
