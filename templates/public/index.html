Our Food Safety Commitment

ShareMeal does not treat surplus food lightly. Every listing requires explicit preparation timestamps, storage condition declarations, packaging condition checks, and allergen labeling.

Strict Expiry Cut-offs
Donations past their collection deadline are automatically expired and blocked from request.
Immediate Quarantine
Any reported temperature or sanitation issue immediately freezes the listing for admin review.
Good Samaritan Protection
Donors acting in good faith comply with municipal standards under established liability protection statutes.
