Step 1

Donor Creates Surplus Listing

A restaurant, bakery, or hotel logs in and submits food details: item name, quantity, preparation timestamp, collection deadline, storage conditions (refrigerated, hot-held, ambient), packaging type, and allergen declarations.

Status: Available
Steps 2 & 3

Organization Requests & Donor Approves

A verified shelter or community kitchen discovers the listing and requests the specific portions they can distribute. The donor reviews the receiving organization's profile and confirms the request. The quantity is reserved atomically to prevent over-allocation.

Status: Request Received → Confirmed (Reserved)
Steps 4 & 5

Volunteer Assigned & Food Picked Up

A verified volunteer accepts the dispatch task and receives direct donor contact and loading dock instructions. At the donor kitchen, the volunteer verifies packaging integrity and collection temperature before confirming pickup.

Status: Volunteer Assigned → Picked Up
Steps 6, 7 & 8

Delivery & Receipt Confirmation

The volunteer delivers the food to the receiving shelter. The receiving supervisor inspects the items, logs arrival temperature and actual received quantity, and signs off. The donation status transitions to Completed, and impact statistics update instantly.

Status: Delivered → Receipt Confirmed → Completed
