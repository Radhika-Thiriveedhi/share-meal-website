Platform Terms of Service & Good Samaritan Liability Shield

Last updated: January 2026

1. Agreement to Terms

By accessing or using the ShareMeal platform, whether as a Donor, Receiving Organization, Volunteer, or General Visitor, you agree to comply with and be bound by these Terms of Service.

2. Role-Based Verification & Accurate Representation

All participating entities must submit authentic verification documents (e.g. food hygiene permits, non-profit tax registration, government identification). Misrepresentation of credentials, organization status, or food conditions constitutes immediate grounds for account termination.

3. Surplus Food Quality & Good Faith Donation

Donors warrant that any food listed was prepared in a licensed commercial kitchen adhering to applicable health codes. Donors shall not donate food known to be spoiled, contaminated, or subject to recall.

4. Statutory Liability Protections

Under applicable food donation laws (including the national Good Samaritan Food Donation Act), donors, non-profit distribution charities, and volunteer couriers acting in good faith are shielded from civil liability, except in cases of gross negligence or intentional misconduct.

5. Dispute Resolution & Safety Audits

ShareMeal administrators reserve the right to audit food handling practices, review temperature logs, and suspend any participant reported for non-compliance with municipal food safety mandates.
